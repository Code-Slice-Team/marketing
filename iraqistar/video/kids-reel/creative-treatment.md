## Audience and purpose

Recruit adult kindergarten/nursery providers and educators to offer live educational classes through IraqiStar Kids. Show how teaching can benefit children, create community, offer earning opportunities, and support the platform's giving promise. Earnings are for adult providers, not children. Applying is followed by document review and approval before teaching children.

The user's standing requirement applies here and to every other reel: include a readable donation message in the actual video, not merely in the social caption.

## Donation accuracy

The Arabic app terms say the platform allocates part of profits from each order to charity in Iraq. The star agreement explicitly says this comes only from the platform's share and does not reduce provider earnings. Source: `messages/ar.json` donation sections; the same promise appears in `catalog.givingBody` and order/session text.

Do not invent a percentage, charity name, donation total, or guarantee that a particular beneficiary receives funds. The reel describes the platform's commitment, not proof of a completed donation. Keep this distinction in future campaigns.
