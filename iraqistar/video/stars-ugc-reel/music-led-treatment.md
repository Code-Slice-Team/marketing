## Soundtrack brief

Contemporary cinematic electronic/house instrumental, 120 BPM, 4/4, minor-key tension resolving into an uplifting final phrase. Tight rounded kick, warm controlled sub-bass, crisp dry clap, syncopated hats, understated hand percussion, restrained plucked synth motif, airy texture and short cinematic impacts. Editorial, stylish and energetic rather than aggressive. No vocals, speech, chants, vocal samples or artist imitation.

Target arrangement: 0–4s immediate rhythmic hook; 4–8s build; 8–32s full groove; 32–36s small breakdown; 36–44s final lift; 44–48s resolved ending. Request an original track for this ad. “Trendy” describes the sound direction; do not claim chart/trending status without evidence. Verify the chosen provider's actual usage terms before calling the soundtrack cleared for paid promotion. No music has yet been generated or licensed.

Mix: music is the lead. Layer only a few tasteful shutter, tap and swish sounds. Avoid excessive cinematic booms. Target a clear, full mobile-speaker mix with no clipping; verify actual loudness and true peak after rendering. Export a silent picture master as well, so the track can be replaced without re-editing the image.

## Visual treatment and branding

- Faces, hands, phones and real creator activity lead the film. Warm directional light, deep neutral shadows, restrained violet accents, shallow depth of field and gentle film grain.
- Main copy: IBM Plex Sans Arabic Bold or Semibold, clean white on dark footage with subtle neutral backing when necessary. No broken Arabic letter animations. URL/wordmark remain LTR; Arabic remains RTL.
- Use the actual `public/brand/iraqistar-lockup-white.svg` and existing brand mark. Fixed violet `#6430f0`, ink `#0c0b16`, white `#ffffff`. Keep logo proportions, clear space and orientation. No redrawn, spinning or mirrored logo.
- Caption headline area should stay above platform controls, normally around the lower-middle of the frame. Keep faces unobscured. Confirm actual legibility in a phone preview with Instagram overlays.
- Most supporting footage cuts last 1–2 seconds. Use cut-on-action, match cuts, a restrained whip, and occasional push-ins. Preserve four-second readable story phrases. No flashing transitions.
- Generated actors are fictional campaign illustrations. Do not add names, testimonials, follower counts, earnings figures or assertions that these people are IraqiStar members.
- No fake app screens. Access code is still pending. Screens are optional inserts, so cinematic footage creation need not wait for site access.
