## Objective

Encourage Iraqi Stars and UGC creators to submit a talent application. This is a recruitment ad, not a fan purchasing ad. Primary action: «قدّم هسه». Intended destination: `https://iraqistar.com/ar/apply`.

The public destination currently displays an invitation-code launch screen (checked in the browser on 2026-09-23). Confirm a publicly accessible application destination before distributing the ad. Do not change site access as part of this creative task.

## Concept

**عندك جمهور؟ لو عندك عين للمحتوى؟**

Two entry points into the same platform: a Star makes a personal moment for a fan; a creator makes useful, engaging content for a business. Use recognizable Iraqi people and everyday settings, natural performances, and tightly edited product actions.

Proposed visual direction: creator footage + real app screens + bold Arabic motion typography. This remains a proposal pending the user's preference. Keep the brand visible from the opening frame; avoid a separate slow logo introduction.

## Voiceover, continuous read

عندك جمهور؟ لو عندك عين للمحتوى؟

خلّي موهبتك تفتحلك باب شغل، ويّه نجم العراق.

إذا إنت نجم، قرّب من جمهورك بفيديو خاص: تهنئة، معايدة، أو كلمة تفرّح واحد يحبّك.

وإذا تصنع محتوى، صوّر للبراندات بطريقتك. منتج تجرّبه، مكان تزوره، أو فكرة تصوّرها من يمّك.

اختار طريقة الشغل اللي تناسبك، وقدّم اهتمامك بالطلبات المناسبة إلك.

طلباتك، فيديوهاتك، ومتابعة أرباحك… بمكان واحد، من موبايلك.

قدّم طلبك، وفريقنا يتواصل وياك. وبعد القبول، تجهّز ملفك.

نجم العراق. عندك جمهور، أو عندك فكرة؟ قدّم هسه.

Voice: native Iraqi speaker, natural Baghdad-leaning delivery, warm confidence, conversational pace. Do not imitate a celebrity. Leave short breaths around «نجم العراق» and the final CTA. Target roughly 135–150 words/minute; actual delivery determines final timing. Full timed captions accompany the voiceover; the table's main screen text is a separate emphasis layer.

## Art and edit direction

- Vertical master: 1080 × 1920, 30fps, target 55 seconds, hard maximum 60 seconds. Capture source footage at higher resolution when available for clean reframing.
- Visual quality comes from good lighting, believable performances, clear UI crops, and precise typography. Use mixed adult Iraqi creators across performance, lifestyle, food and product content. Keep locations contemporary and locally plausible.
- Most footage cuts: 0.7–1.8 seconds; allow 2–3 seconds for an important app action. Opening cuts can be faster. Use straight cuts, match cuts, one restrained whip transition, and short speed ramps. No rapid flashing.
- Brand palette: neutral ink `#0c0b16` and white, with `#6430f0` for the logo/action accents. No violet full-screen decorative fields. Use `public/brand/iraqistar-lockup.svg` or the white version according to background contrast.
- Preserve the mark, horizontal lockup, clear space and original proportions. The logo never spins or mirrors; do not turn it into a transition wipe. A separate camera movement can create the transition.
- IBM Plex Sans Arabic Semibold/Bold for Arabic, Geist for Latin. Keep captions to two short lines where possible. Use solid or sufficiently opaque neutral caption backing over footage. Do not animate individual Arabic letters in ways that break shaping.
- Keep essential captions, logos and CTA inside a conservative central area; preview with the actual Instagram Reel/ad overlays before delivery. This is an editing choice, not a claim about fixed platform safe-zone dimensions.
- Music: proposed 110–120 BPM contemporary instrumental with subtle Iraqi percussion, sourced with advertising rights. Beat cuts and a few tactile camera/tap sounds; duck music under speech. Do not depend on trending audio being cleared for promotion.
- Delivery target: H.264 MP4 with AAC stereo, plus a clean version without burned captions and a captioned version. Proposed video bitrate 15–20 Mbps; preserve a higher-quality source master. Verify on a phone for Arabic legibility, voice intelligibility, synchronization and the full final URL.

## Footage and app-screen list

1. Adult Star speaking into a phone in a clean, warm setting.
2. Adult UGC creator preparing a simple tabletop product shot.
3. Product demonstration/unboxing with no unrelated prominent brand marks.
4. Café or store visit being filmed.
5. Home creator setup with phone/tripod and natural light.
6. Brief reaction to a personal message, staged or cleared for use.
7. Creator profile, UGC brief, creator studio, and talent application screens captured from an authorized review environment. No need to access admin/customer records for this ad.
8. Supplied logo SVGs and local brand font files.

Existing reference: `public/teaser.mp4` is a 20-second, 1080×1920, 60fps soft-launch teaser. Sampled frames show Arabic motion typography; it has not been altered or counted as a new recruitment-video deliverable.

For AI-generated footage, use generic adult actors, not lookalikes of real Stars. Generate clean footage without logos, Arabic text, or app UI; composite the real brand and verified screens during editing. Do not present generated actors as existing IraqiStar members. For real footage, select material whose participants and usage rights cover promotional use.

## Inspiration and rationale

This concept borrows positioning patterns, not specific ad assets or proven performance claims:

- [Cameo enrollment](https://www.cameo.com/enroll): open with the value of a Star's personal connection with fans; demonstrate recording a requested message.
- [Billo for creators](https://billo.app/for-creators/): show the act of creating brand content as the opportunity. Do not import its payout or eligibility promises.
- [JoinBrands creator guide](https://help.joinbrands.com/en/articles/6462008-creators-getting-started-guide): make joining and working visually understandable in a few steps, while retaining IraqiStar's actual approval process.
