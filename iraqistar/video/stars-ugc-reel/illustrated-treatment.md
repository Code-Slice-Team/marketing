## Visual execution

Deep neutral ink backgrounds, subtle depth, soft card shadows, dimensional vector-style objects, restrained violet accents and crisp white typography. All lettering uses the project's font files: IBM Plex Sans Arabic and Geist. Arabic is rendered with native text shaping. The brand mark is parsed from the existing SVG path; the wordmark follows the existing SVG's Geist size/spacing and horizontal composition. The logo is never mirrored or rotated.

All visual objects are editorial illustrations. Document/portfolio cards are not actual app screenshots and contain no fabricated personal data, prices, earnings, member counts, or claims of completed applications. No access-code-dependent screens are required to render the film.

## Original soundtrack

Original 120 BPM synthesized electronic instrumental: rounded kick, syncopated hats, dry clap, warm bass, minor-key chord bed, plucked melody, stereo delays and short percussion fills. Build into 8 seconds, main groove through 32, small breakdown at 32–36, final lift, then resolved ending. No vocals, third-party songs, external samples, or artist imitation. “Contemporary/trendy” is a style choice, not a claim that this is a currently trending released track.

The music was composed specifically for this artifact. Keep the silent master if the user later chooses a different approved track for Instagram. No Runway account or other generation connection was used for this version.

## Production and validation

Rendered at 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 48 seconds. Video uses H.264/yuv420p with fast-start metadata; the finished reel uses AAC stereo audio. The audio master is 48 kHz, 24-bit stereo WAV. Check results are saved in `render-checks.txt`.

All twelve representative scene frames were visually inspected for layout, Arabic shaping, contrast and text overflow before full rendering. The final exported video is separately checked for stream properties, decoding errors, loudness and correct end-card text.

The work only adds/updates marketing artifacts and project notes. Rendering/composition helper scripts are outside the repository under `/private/tmp/iraqistar-reel-build/`. No application source, dependencies, database, site access settings or social posts were changed by this task. Other concurrent workspace changes are unrelated and were left alone.

The public application link displayed an invitation gate during the earlier site review; verify public accessibility before running the ad. The video itself is ready for review without changing that gate.
